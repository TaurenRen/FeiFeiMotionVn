Effects for OM.sfx(name, at, { gain, pan, pitch, note, dur }):
  pop      a small element appears (note: scale degree)
  tick     tiny UI tick, a bar or row arriving
  click    mouse or button click
  whoosh   something sweeps past (0.5 s, peaks on the hit)
  whip     fast camera whip / slide-out
  thud     a card or object lands on the desk
  impact   a title slams in (big, use once or twice)
  paper    paper, a sticky note or photo slapped down
  stamp    a rubber stamp thumps down
  ding     a bell: something is right / done (note: scale degree)
  chime    two rising bell notes: a reveal, the end card
  sparkle  glitter: a highlight, a star
  count    a number counting up (dur; dense then slowing, like expo.out)
  riser    a swell that ends on the hit (dur)
  draw     a marker or pen stroke (dur) — underlines, circles
  type     keyboard typing (dur)
  boing    a spring: a jump, a playful reaction
  slide    slide whistle falling (dur)
  hit      a short punch for a hard cut on the beat (kick + click; tech montages)
  subdrop  an 808 sub falling away under a drop or a big reveal (dur)
  reverse  a reversed cymbal swelling into the hit (dur; ends on `at`)
  glitch   a digital stutter burst: a glitch cut, data corrupting, a screen switching
  shutter  camera shutter: a photo, a screenshot
